# Ethics, legal posture and misuse boundaries

T.O.C.A.I.A is built for defensive intelligence, research, training and reproducible analysis of legitimately accessible open-source information.

## Public reference posture

The public implementation is intentionally passive:

- no messaging, following, liking or contacting a target;
- no credential harvesting or bypass of access controls;
- no automation intended to defeat CAPTCHAs, rate limits or anti-bot controls;
- no automatic identity attribution from weak signals;
- no psychological diagnosis or remote psychometric scoring;
- no real-person stalking, doxxing or location-prediction demonstrations.

## Data minimization

Public examples use synthetic data. If contributors submit case material, they must remove direct personal contact information and unnecessary personal identifiers, document lawful basis and minimize retention.

## Analytical safeguards

- Separate account, operator and person as units of analysis.
- Treat source dependence as a first-class property.
- Record collection failures rather than converting them into behavioral conclusions.
- Declare uncertainty and revision criteria.
- Prefer the least invasive method that can answer the requirement.

## Not legal advice

Openly accessible information is not automatically unrestricted for every purpose. Applicable privacy, data-protection, professional and sector rules depend on jurisdiction and context. Obtain appropriate legal review for operational use.
