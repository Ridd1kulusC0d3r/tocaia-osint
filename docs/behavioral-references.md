Consulted 2026-10-12. These sources inform methodological choices; none validates T.O.C.A.I.A or endorses its implementation.

## Project decisions derived from these principles

The observation/inference/forecast separation, profile template and evaluation gates are project design decisions. Their operational utility remains unmeasured. Do not present a checklist, ACH matrix, Brier score or visual identity as proof of scientific validation.

No claim is made that posting patterns reliably expose personality, mental health, hidden intent, identity or physical whereabouts. Any future behavioral model needs a defined outcome, suitable ground truth, temporal validation, comparison with simple baselines and documented failure modes.
