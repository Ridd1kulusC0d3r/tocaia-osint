# Methodological references

The framework draws on established intelligence-analysis and digital open-source investigation principles. These references are starting points, not an attempt to claim endorsement of T.O.C.A.I.A.

- Heuer, Richards J. *Psychology of Intelligence Analysis*. Center for the Study of Intelligence, 1999.
- Heuer, Richards J.; Pherson, Randolph H. *Structured Analytic Techniques for Intelligence Analysis*. 3rd ed., 2020.
- Kent, Sherman. “Words of Estimative Probability.” *Studies in Intelligence*, 1964.
- Office of the Director of National Intelligence. *ICD 203: Analytic Standards*.
- Office of the High Commissioner for Human Rights / UC Berkeley Human Rights Center. *Berkeley Protocol on Digital Open Source Investigations*, 2022.
- Youyou, Wu; Kosinski, Michal; Stillwell, David. “Computer-based personality judgments are more accurate than those made by humans.” *PNAS*, 2015. Included here as background on digital traces, not as permission for remote diagnosis.

When a project claim depends on a specific paper, standard or legal rule, the relevant file should cite the exact source rather than relying on this general list.
