## Status vocabulary

- `methodological invariant`: design rule, not an empirical performance claim.
- `synthetic-only`: result observed only in simulated data.
- `reported-real-data`: project notes report real-data evidence, but the public reproduction package is not yet available here.
- `refuted in project notes`: tested claim that did not survive the stated experiment.
- `limited`: narrow evidence that must not be generalized into a verdict.
- `unmeasured`: no defensible performance claim yet.
